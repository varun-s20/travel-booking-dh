# Deconstruction: Kasa (https://kasa.com)
Scope: homepage hero and its four-field search, header, the 390 search and menu, a results page (Austin location page), homepage sections below the hero and the footer, motion; plus "Hotel page, additions". Measured 2026-11-10, at 1440x900 (homepage height 8454, results 3693) and 390x844 (homepage 10640, results 7710).
Path: homepage, then the footer link to the Austin location page (kasa.com/locations/austin-tx) for results. The search form was never submitted and no Search button was pressed. I opened the four fields, and picked Nov 18 to Nov 20 in the date pickers (hero, results bar, and the hotel page's own "Select dates" dialog). The one confirm control I pressed was "Update" in the hotel page dialog, which re-routed that property page to a dated URL (/search?checkInDate=...) and showed prices. No sign-in, no personal details, no booking.
Cookie banner (bottom right, "Accept all" and "Preferences" plus an X): closed with the X only at 1440. At 390 it had not appeared.
The 390 view is desktop Chromium resized to 390, not a phone: hover rules and mouse scripts still ran, touch was not emulated.
Values are evidence for how the system is built, not for reuse. No code, images, text blocks or font files saved. Screens are in `kasa/` next to this file. The hotel detail page is in `projects/travel-booking/references/deconstruct/kasa-hotel-detail.md` and is not repeated here.

Stack facts: Next.js with SCSS CSS modules (class names like `HeroSection-module-scss-module__...`), react-day-picker (`rdp-` classes), Google Maps on results, hero media is Cloudinary-hosted mp4. No GSAP or Lenis seen on these pages.

## Type (with the licence of each family)

## Color system
- Ground rgb(247,246,242); surface rgb(253,252,251); text and primary action navy rgb(6,19,50). Navy alpha ramp as the secondary system: 0.8 secondary text, 0.6 disabled, 0.2 search-card border, 0.1 hovers and hairlines, 0.05 chip and tile fills.
- Green rgb(14,133,75): review score and the "Best value" badge fill. Orange rgb(195,86,2): the "Only 1 left" scarcity line (14/500). These are the only accents. Call to action is always the solid navy button.
- Hero scrim (the most important part for a full-photo hero), four layers over the video:
  1. A flat navy wash on the slide content layer: rgba(6,19,50,0.20), full hero size.
  2. A top fade: `linear-gradient(black, transparent)`, 500px tall from y 0, layer opacity 0.6 (so black at 60% alpha fading to 0 over 500px). It sits under the headline and darkens behind the nav and H1.
  3. A bottom fade per slide: `linear-gradient(transparent, black)`, 164px tall at the bottom edge, layer opacity 0.15 (very light; it just seats the credit line and the pause button).
  4. The navbar's own frosted strip: rgba(253,252,251,0.10) with `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`, 66px tall, over the video.
  No mid-hero darkening: the lower 540px of the photo is only washed by the 20% navy.
- White search card on the photo: surface fill, 0.8px border rgba(6,19,50,0.2), shadow `0 14px 16px -2px rgba(0,0,0,0.03)` (barely visible on a photo; the card separates by value and the hairline, not by shadow).

## Motion
- Hero media: a crossfade carousel of ten muted videos, each about 5.04s long, played once (no loop attribute). Slides cross-fade; I sampled opacity every 100ms and a fade took about 600 to 700ms, roughly linear, with the next change at about 4.9s after the previous. Each slide shows a different property and the credit line at bottom-left changes with it. A "Pause carousel" button (34px round, surface fill, bottom-right) stops it. Videos have no poster attribute; the first frame is an image from the same Cloudinary source (`so_0` frame) that sits under the video.
- Hero load sequence: I could not capture the first 600ms frame by frame, so the entrance of the H1 and search card is not measured (see Not measured). Settled state is at once the full hero, no loader.
- Navbar: `transform 0.4s cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.2, 0.64, 1)` (small overshoot), background and shadow 0.25s. Transparent frosted strip over the hero; after scrolling past the hero it turns solid surface rgb(253,252,251) with a `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)` shadow and navy text. Scroll down hides it, any scroll up brings it back with the bar expanded.
- Search card fields: `height 0.2s`, background 0.15s ease-in-out, border-color 0.05s ease-in-out; the Search button `width, height, background, color 0.2s`; dividers `height 0.2s, border-color 0.25s ease-in-out`.
- Popover panels grow out of the card as one surface (see Interactions); height transition 0.2s.
- Hover and focus (default and hover pair saved): field hover fills the field rgba(6,19,50,0.10) (radius 8, no border); Search button hover goes from rgb(6,19,50) to rgb(31,43,71) with no shadow; list option hover fills the row rgba(6,19,50,0.10) and the icon tile darkens; focus on the destination input draws a 0.8px teal border rgb(53,150,150) plus a 4px outer ring in navy at 0.2 (the only teal on the site, used for focus). Nav links change on `0.05s ease-out`.
- Homepage heading reveal and section motion are the same word-blur system recorded in the hotel file (not re-measured).
- Reduced motion (emulated `reduce`, homepage): section headings render at opacity 1 with no blur or transform (reveal collapsed). The hero carousel still advanced between slides within 6.5s and the navbar transform transition stayed at 0.4s, so the hero crossfade and the navbar overshoot are not disabled by the preference on the homepage. Results page reduced-motion not tested.
- Scroll: the map column on results is sticky. Nothing is scroll-scrubbed.

## Signature techniques (3 to 5, rebuildable from scratch)
1. **Full-viewport video carousel with a light, layered scrim.** A 100vh, full-bleed section holding ten stacked `<video muted playsinline>` slides (each about 5s, played once, no loop), absolutely positioned and cross-faded by toggling opacity over about 650ms on a 5s timer; the property credit and a pause button sit bottom-left and bottom-right. The scrim is not one gradient: a flat navy wash at 20%, a 500px top gradient from black (layer opacity 0.6) fading to clear, a 164px bottom gradient at 0.15 opacity, and a 66px frosted nav strip (surface at 10% with 6px blur). That keeps the photo bright and still reads off-white text at 56px.
2. **A search card that sits under the headline, not on the lower edge.** A 916x60 surface card (radius 16, 0.8px navy 20% border, 5px padding) centred under the subline at about one third of the hero height, holding four 48px fields (radius 8) with 1px, 40px hairline dividers and a navy Search button. The photo gets 540px of breathing room below it. This is the opposite of a docked-bottom pattern; the headline, subline and card read as one centred stack.
3. **Popovers that extend the card instead of floating.** When a field opens, the same white surface grows downward (panel flush under the card at the same width and radius, height eased 0.2s), so the card and its panel are one object. The active field gets a navy outline and grey tint; each panel ends in a hairline and one underlined "Clear". Panels: a scrolling location list with 42px icon tiles, a two-month range calendar (40px circles, solid navy ends, navy 10% in-between band), stepper rows (34px round minus and plus), and radio rows. On a phone the same content becomes a full-screen sheet with accordion cards and a fixed Clear all / Search footer.
4. **Scroll-aware nav and pinned compact search (hero version).** The nav is a frosted strip over the video and turns solid with a hairline shadow after the hero; the hero card scrolls away and a compact 852x52 pinned bar takes over on the way down, expanding again on any upward scroll, driven by a 0.4s `cubic-bezier(0.34,1.2,0.64,1)` transform. Booking is always one gesture away.
5. **Split results with a sticky map and quiet price rules.** A 680px list and 664px sticky map (stacked, map first, at 390); cards with a single price rule: until dates exist, a navy "Add dates for prices" button stands in for price; once dates exist, show total (underlined, 18/500), nightly (14, secondary), a struck-through original at 60%, an orange scarcity line, and a green "Best value" pill. Serif for H1 and H2s only, sans 400 and 500 for everything else, navy alpha for all secondary styles.

Landfall auditions: type and palette

Checkpoint 2a for the Chief Officer. Read with landfall/design/concepts.md. The photo is Landfall's hero (Lisbon from Alfama over the Tagus, Unsplash License, Lisha Riabinina) under Kasa's layered scrim. The headline and subline are working copy for judging type only; prices come from the shared mock data. "Landfall" is a placeholder name.

Type options (all three are SIL OFL; Kasa's own Tiempos Headline and TT Norms Pro are commercial and cannot ship)

Each option shows the hero at 1440 proportions, then a section heading, the price rule and a flight leg with the landfall line. Kasa sets headings in one regular weight, sentence case, no tracking; these follow that.

# travel-booking: benchmark rubric (Varun's ruling, 2026-11-11)

For this project only, the Critic scores each design side by side against the client's three named competitors: Booking.com, Expedia and Priceline. The per-page inventory of what they offer is in references/benchmark.md (built from the deconstructions in references/deconstruct/). Also report the studio score from taste/rubric.md, for reference only.

Target: 9.2 or more for each design.

## Criteria and weights
| Criterion | Weight | The question |
|---|---|---|
| Depth and detail | 25% | Page by page, does it offer as many useful options, filters, facts and states as the three, laid out with the precision of the moodboard picks? |
| Ease of use | 20% | Is the search prominent and quick, is everything easy to find, does switching Flights and Hotels change the homepage in place, and do the flows run without friction? |
| Visual design and imagery | 20% | Is it one strong identity carried through every section and page, with strong travel photography, and finished beyond what the three manage? |
| Craft | 15% | Is it clean at 1440, 1024 and 390 and at every scroll position, with hover and focus on everything interactive? |
| Pricing clarity | 10% | Are totals, what they include, per-person and per-night prices, and changes always clear and consistent? |
| Mobile | 10% | Is the 390 experience as complete and polished as the desktop one? |

Weighted total = 0.25 x depth + 0.20 x ease + 0.20 x visual + 0.15 x craft + 0.10 x pricing + 0.10 x mobile.

## Anchors (every criterion)
- 9-10: on every page checked it matches or beats the strongest of the three, and adds a finish and identity none of them has.
- 7-8: matches the weakest of the three; some pages or sections are thinner or plainer.
- 5-6: noticeably thinner than all three: fewer options, less information, sections that feel like placeholders.
- 1-3: missing pages or broken flows.

## Caps and checks
- Design tells (taste/anti-slop.md): one caps visual design at 5, three or more at 3. Copy tells block the target until fixed.
- Overlap, clipped text or a header collision caps craft at 7 (as in taste/rubric.md).
- Shared design with the other version (layout skeleton, components, type, colour roles, photos, icons, motion) caps visual design at 6.
- A section that reads as filler (repeated facts, an empty or thin band, a footer with too few links) caps depth at 7.
- Score what you can see and use in the browser. Name the competitor and page you compared against for every score under 9.

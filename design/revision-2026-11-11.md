# Overwing revision for the client's feedback (2026-11-11)
Art Director, art-direct skill phase B, for the Developer. Apply in order, page by page, to projects/travel-booking/site/. Every value is in design/tokens.css (copy it over site/app/styles/tokens.css) and is shown working in prototype/index.html (navy by default, ?palette=cabin for Cabin dawn).

What the client asked for (brief.md, "Client feedback, 2026-11-11"): deep navy, white and teal with a clean, readable font; a prominent search; easy navigation; clear pricing; strong travel imagery; a polished mobile experience; the ease of Booking.com, Expedia and Priceline with its own identity. What stays: the idea (the cabin window), Hipcamp as the primary reference (rail, pill tab bar, the search pill and thumb), Calibre plus Geist, the copy deck and the simulated backend. Booking.com, Expedia and Priceline are ease-of-use benchmarks only: how search, prices and the mobile flow work, never their look.

Overwing's own take, in one line: the cabin at night. A deep navy wall holds the search, and its windows are lit by a coast at first light, edged in teal.

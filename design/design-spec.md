# Design spec: travel-booking (working name Overwing, a placeholder)
Primary reference: Hipcamp (hipcamp.com/en-GB). Section donors: Kasa: hotel detail page. Monocle City Guides: popular destinations cards. Faye: fare and room inclusion cards. The Hoxton: hotel search fields (labels used on both tabs, Varun's ruling 6) and region pills.

Art Director, 2026-11-10, phase B. Built on Varun's choices in design/choices.md: concept A Overwing, type T1 (Calibre plus Geist), two palette versions from one codebase (P3 Harbour gold, default, and P1 Cabin dawn), stock photography across the whole site.

Files: design/tokens.css (every value; plain CSS custom properties), prototype/ (navigation and hero, built in Varun's structure; open prototype/index.html, add ?palette=cabin for Cabin dawn), prototype/tools/ (asset, grade, token-sync and contrast scripts).

Placeholders, said once and meant everywhere: "Overwing", its wordmark and window glyph, the market defaults (English, USD, no fee beyond the fare's taxes and fees), every contact detail, every fare, flight and hotel, and the headline copy are placeholders or simulated data, not client facts. The Copywriter writes the real copy into content/copy.md.

## 1. Concept
Hipcamp's system, a quiet app shell around one tool-first white page, becomes Overwing's by looking out of the window. The client has no brand, no media and no market yet, so the one idea that belongs to this business is the view you only get on a flight: the hush after take-off, clouds below, in good hands. The page stays Hipcamp's (rail, centred sentence-case headline, Flights / Hotels toggle, the 72px search pill with a lifting white thumb, short calm state changes) and the brand lives in one shape, the cabin window: a tall rounded rectangle with its blind half down. In the hero, four windows run across the column as one continuous wing-over-clouds view; on load their blinds rise one after another, and on scroll the view drifts sideways a little slower than the page. The window then returns in exactly five places (destination cards, the searching state, the wordmark's o, chip glyphs, the app tile) and stays out of the hotel pages, which keep Kasa's photo grid. Everything else is restraint: one tint as the only interactive surface, one deep colour for every call to action, one accent for rings and glyphs, and one colour grade over all studio media. Both palettes run the same layout, copy, imagery and motion.

## 2c. Image direction
- One grade over every studio and stock image: the palette's SVG filter (--fx-grade). Harbour gold warms the light to late gold and cools the shadows to sea; Cabin dawn barely moves the photo it was sampled from. Duffel's hotel photos and airline logos are never graded (they must look as supplied); in the simulated build the stock stand-ins for them are treated as Duffel's.
- Crop by focal point (object-position from data), never centre by default.
- One ratio per section: hero windows 236:318 (one panorama photo, about 3:2 source); entry cards 2:1; destination cards 1:1 inside the window mask; trust panel photo 4:5 at 1440 and 3:2 at 390; hotel results 1:1; hotel grid fixed tracks (Kasa); room cards 3:2; dashboard thumbs 1:1; about 21:9 and 3:2; inner banners full bleed.
- Full bleed only for the strongest shots: the hero view, the About banner, the footer's faint photo.
- Never a small image floating in a large empty area: images fill their slot edge to edge, inside the slot's radius.
- Subjects: real places, light and travel moments; people small and turned away or in motion (no model releases on Unsplash); no airline liveries, logos or hotel brand names in shot; no famous-landmark postcard shots for destinations (street life and light instead, Monocle's rule of place plus a person).
- Sources: Unsplash License only (not Unsplash+), checked on each photo page, credits kept in assets/images/credits.md (photographer, URL, date checked). Replace with client or licensed media when it arrives.
- Sizes: WebP derivatives (prototype/tools/assets.py), never upscaled; the hero source is 2340 x 1540 after a 60px crop of the camera's own window frame.

## 3. Tokens
All values in design/tokens.css; this is the summary.
- Palette roles (both palettes, same names): --c-accent (rings, glyphs, selected marks; never small text), --c-accent-soft (accent on dark, the glyph's pane), --c-deep (every call to action, selected calendar ends, dark bands), --c-ink (text), --c-muted (secondary text, placeholders, icons), --c-tint (the one interactive surface), --c-ground (page), --c-cream (quiet panels), --c-surface (white: pill, popovers, cards), --c-line (decorative hairlines), --c-field (3:1 field borders), --c-bezel, --c-blind, --c-blind-end, --c-mat, --c-extra, --c-scrim, --fx-grade. Shared state colours: --c-error, --c-success, --c-warn and their tints.
- Harbour gold (default): accent #9B6438, accent-soft #E0B378, deep #1D3A4E, ink #0F1D27, muted #4E6070, tint #EEE4D5, line #E4DACB, ground #FAF5EE, cream #F3EBDF, mat #E9D2B3, extra #E6EEF2. Ratios: ink on ground 15.8, ink on tint 13.6, muted on ground 6.0 / white 6.5 / tint 5.2, white on deep 11.9, accent ring on ground 4.5 / white 4.9 / tint 3.9, gold on deep 6.2, ink on mat 11.7 (muted on mat 4.4: not allowed). The tint and line were cool blue-grey (#E6EEF2, #DCE5EA) until critic round 1 found them clashing with the sand; they are now sand-derived, and the sea tint survives only as --c-extra (paid extras).
- Cabin dawn: accent #278B9B, accent-soft #A9D3DA, deep #1F3B41, ink #121D20, muted #5B6567, tint #F4E9E7, ground #FBF8F7, cream #EED7D5, mat #A9D3DA. Ratios: ink on ground 16.3, muted on ground 5.7 / white 6.0 / tint 5.0, white on deep 11.9, accent ring 3.8 on ground / 4.0 on white / 3.4 on tint (rings and glyphs only, never text under 24px, never white text on it), muted on cream 4.4 (ink only on cream).
- Measured on the built hero (prototype/tools/sample_contrast.py, every pixel behind each text box with the text hidden, element opacity blended in; 1440 and 390, both palettes, plus a scrolled 1440 x 700 capture; five captures): every text element passes; lowest 5.0:1 (Cabin dawn inactive toggle label) and 5.2:1 in Harbour gold, headline 15.8 to 16.3:1, fact chips 7.3:1. No text sits on media anywhere in the hero, so no scrim was needed. Re-measured after round 2's tint change; not re-measured after round 3 (only the photo grade and positions changed there, and no text sits on the photo).
- Grade (tools/grade_filter.py): Harbour gold mixes 34% of a gradient map (ink, sea deep, warm grey #7C7570 at .48, gold #D2A06C at .64, #F0CFA3 at .84, #FFF6E8) over the photo at 94% saturation; tuned twice after the critic found it first grey, then rose with olive cloud tops. Cabin dawn mixes 18% of a blush and slate map.

## 5. Motion
- State changes (Hipcamp): colours and backgrounds 150ms --ease-std; segment tint 260ms; thumbs 220ms --ease-spring; popovers pop-in 160ms (opacity and 6px rise); accordions 200ms ease-out; card images 1.05 over 250ms; sheets on the phone open instantly.
- The one load moment (Overwing): on the home page the four blinds rise from closed to 79% up, 1600ms --ease-out-long, 240ms after first paint, 120ms apart, released once the view image has loaded (1.2s cap, so the view is never held back). The search is usable from the first frame; nothing else enters.
- The drift (Overwing): the view moves sideways 0.12px per px scrolled, capped at 6% of its width, through one passive scroll listener batched in requestAnimationFrame and paused when the hero is off screen.
- The searching state's drifting window is the only looping animation, and only while a search runs.
- Compact search bar (Kasa): 400ms --ease-spring position, 200ms size.
- Not used: masked line reveals, word blur reveals (Kasa), scroll-scrubbed card fans (Faye), page wipes. Varun ruling 7 limits entrance motion to the blinds.
- Reduced motion: blinds start raised, no drift, no thumb slides, popovers appear without the rise, button breathing off, the searching window static, smooth scrolling off. Checked in the prototype with the media setting emulated.

# Deconstruction: Hipcamp (https://www.hipcamp.com/en-GB)
Scope: whole site (primary reference for project travel-booking). Measured: 2026-11-10, at 1440x900 and 390x844 (desktop Chromium, so 390 is a narrow window, not a phone: hover rules and mouse scripts still run, no touch emulation).
Pages visited: homepage, results page (Lake District, reached by clicking the homepage "Lake District" card), listing detail (reached by clicking the first result card), the 390 map-search route (reached by the "Map" button). Checkout was not reached.
Screens are in `projects/travel-booking/references/deconstruct/hipcamp/` (names below). Scout screens in `references/screens/hipcamp-*.png` were read first and are not repeated.
Values are evidence for how the system is built, not for reuse. No code, images, text or font files were saved. Popups: the "See this Hipcamp in... (Open / Continue)" app sheet appeared on the listing page at 390 and blocked the view; I dismissed it with "Continue" (no account created). Nothing else was closed or accepted.

Stack facts: Next.js (pages router), styled-components (legacy parts, classes `sc-...`) plus Tailwind (newer parts: rail, search pill, tab toggle, popovers, mobile nav, top strip). No animation library found (no GSAP, Framer Motion, Lenis, Swiper globals). Map is Mapbox on listing pages. Date picker is react-day-picker (`rdp-*` classes). Menus and the top panel are Headless UI style (`data-closed`, `data-leave`).

## Motion

Almost no entrance motion. Hipcamp feels quiet because the only things that animate are state changes, and they are short.
- On load: nothing animates in. Content shows a skeleton shimmer (`skeletonShimmer`, background-position sweep) only while data loads. No hero entrance, no staggered reveals.
- On scroll: no scroll-triggered reveals found. Images lazy load (`loading=lazy`). Things that react to scroll: the header controls collapse into a floating pill cluster; on the listing page a sticky 80px section tab bar (Overview / Units / Reviews / Amenities / Location with active underline, plus an orange "Select a unit" button at the right) appears when the booking card leaves view; the map on results is sticky; at 390 the floating tab bar fades out on the listing page (opacity transition 0.2s) and a fixed booking bar takes its place.
- Hover (computed):
  - Pill segment: background `#f3f1ea` (warm tint), neighbouring divider fades to opacity 0 (`opacity 0.2s cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)`), segment transition 0.26s `cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)` on flex-grow, opacity, padding, background-color, box-shadow.
  - Search button: `hover:brightness-95`, 0.15s.
  - Homepage tile: lifts `translateY(-3px)` with a small shadow, `transform .2s ease-in-out, box-shadow .2s ease-in-out`. Text color `0.25s`.
  - Result card: inner card background goes from transparent to `#f3f1ea`, `background-color .15s, box-shadow .15s`; the matching map pin swaps from white fill (dark text) to olive fill (white text); pin button has `transform .1s` (scale feel).
  - Popover rows: `hover:bg-raised`, 0.15s color/background.
- Tab toggle: the white thumb is an absolutely positioned span that slides: `left, width, transform .22s cubic-bezier(.34,1.28,.64,1)` (slight overshoot spring). Label color changes `0.15s cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)`.
- Active-segment thumb in the pill: when any field is opened, an absolutely positioned white span (shadow-md, full radius) moves to that segment: `left, width, transform, opacity .22s cubic-bezier(.34,1.28,.64,1)`. The rest of the pill dims to the warm tint (the pill background turns `#f3f1ea`). Where field expands (flex-grow 2) while Dates and Campers compress.
- Popovers (destination, dates, campers): `ulPopEnter` 0.16s `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`, from `opacity 0, translateY(-6px)` to rest.
- "Why Hipcamp?" top panel: scrim fades 200ms; panel `transition 300ms ease-out`, closed state `-translate-y-8` + `opacity 0`; leave uses `ease-in`.
- 390 "More" bottom sheet: measured by sampling every 30ms after the tap: appears in one frame (full scrim opacity, sheet at final y, zero Web Animations running). Closing is also instant (gone within 30 to 60ms). So no slide motion at all.
- 390 full-screen "Where to?" sheet: `fixed inset-0 z-1100` white, no animation property.
- Other keyframes defined in the stylesheet (unused on the pages visited): `fadeSlideUp`, `stepIn` (8px rise), `ulRiseIn`, `marqueeLeft/Right`, `float`, `sitePinPulse` and `location-pulse` (pin pulse scale 2.2 to 2.5), `HeroGallery-fill` (story-style progress bar on the listing gallery dots), `SunriseHills-*` and `HeroBackdrop-*` (illustrated hero backdrops from other pages).
- Common durations: 0.15s (Tailwind default) 30 elements, 0.2s ease-in-out 36 elements, 0.25s ease 36 elements, 0.26s, 0.22s spring, 0.3s.
- Libraries: none. All CSS transitions and keyframes.
- prefers-reduced-motion: partly respected. The newer Tailwind pieces use `motion-safe:` and `motion-reduce:` classes: with reduced motion emulated, the tab thumb and pill segments transition-duration became 0s. The older styled-components pieces ignore it: the homepage tile lift still ran 0.2s. Five `prefers-reduced-motion` media rules exist in the stylesheets.

## Signature techniques (3 to 5, rebuildable from scratch)

1. **A tabbed pill search where the active field lifts.** One white pill, 72px tall, full radius, shadow-md, 0.8px warm hairline. Inside, three plain buttons (Where, Dates, Campers) separated by 1px dividers inset 20px top and bottom, plus a 48px orange circle button at the right. Hover: the hovered segment gets the warm tint and its neighbouring dividers fade out (CSS `:has()` on `data-segment`). Click: the pill background turns the warm tint and a single absolutely positioned white span with shadow-md and full radius slides under the active segment with a slightly overshooting curve (`.22s cubic-bezier(.34,1.28,.64,1)` on left, width, transform). The active segment grows (flex-grow 2 vs 1 over .26s) while the others compress. Under it a popover (radius 24, shadow-xl, `ulPopEnter` .16s: 6px rise plus fade) opens. Above the pill, a two-option segmented toggle (track 40px, thumb 32px) uses the same sliding thumb. For travel: Flights / Hotels in the toggle; From, To, Dates, Passengers as the segments; passenger popover as the stepper rows pattern.
2. **A linked list and map.** Two columns (540px list, 660px map, map sticky at top 72px). Each card is wrapped so hover tints the card (`#f3f1ea`, .15s) and simultaneously turns the matching map pin from white to the dark olive with white text. State is shared by id (hover id in a parent store). Cards are full-click targets, with a tapered-dot photo carousel, one action, and no sort control on the list itself. At 390 the map disappears and a floating dark pill (90x43) above the tab bar navigates to a map-first route with a draggable bottom sheet. Flight results would keep the list half and drop the map.
3. **A quiet app shell.** Desktop: a fixed 88px icon rail (24px outline icons over 10px labels, a 40px tinted rounded square marks the current page, More pinned at the bottom) with no divider fills and the page on white; account controls float top right as three pills that gather into a shadowed cluster on scroll. Mobile: the rail becomes a floating pill-shaped tab bar (333x75, radius full, 24px off the bottom, shadow-2xl) with five 28px icons and labels that fades out on the listing page when a fixed booking bar needs the space. Sheets (More menu, filters, sort) open and close with no animation on mobile and with a short fade-and-rise on desktop.
4. **One warm tint as the single interactive surface.** The same warm grey (243,241,234) is the toggle track, the hover and active tint for pill segments, the Log in pill, the popover icon discs, the stepper buttons, the calendar range band, the rail active square, the card hover tint and the hairlines. One tint plus one orange action color plus one dark fill is all the interaction color there is, which is why dense search UI stays calm.
5. **Booking flow built from selectable rows.** Listing detail runs from a sticky 392px booking card (price, two fields, one orange button; opens an in-card calendar), into an in-page "Select a unit" block (dates and guests, chips, option cards with photo, spec line, amenity grid and a price plus one outline button, beside a sticky map), then add-on "Extras" cards with per-item prices, with a sticky section tab bar that turns into an orange "Select a unit" button. Filters use bordered option rows (icon, title, helper) and a footer button that shows the live result count ("Show 86 places"). All of it maps to fare selection, baggage and seat extras, and a filter drawer.

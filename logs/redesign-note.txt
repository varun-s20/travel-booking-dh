travel-booking: redesign note (Studio Lead, 2026-11-11). Read this before anything else. It overrides earlier notes where they conflict.

WHY
The client sent feedback (verbatim in brief.md, "Client feedback, 2026-11-11"):
- A clean, modern travel platform with the ease of use of Booking.com, Expedia and Priceline, but its own distinct identity.
- Deep navy, white and teal, with a clean, readable font.
- Priorities: a prominent search section, easy navigation, clear pricing, strong travel imagery, a polished mobile experience.
Varun also ruled that the two versions must be two completely different designs. Until now they were one layout (Overwing) in two palettes, which was wrong.

THE TWO DESIGNS
1. Overwing (concept A, the cabin windows). Lives where it is now: design/, prototype/, build/, site/.
   - Primary reference Hipcamp, donors Kasa (hotel detail), Monocle, Faye, The Hoxton (as before).
   - Type: Calibre plus Geist (as before).
   - Palettes: a NEW navy, white and teal palette (Overwing's own take on the client's colours), which becomes the default, plus Cabin dawn as the alternate. Harbour gold leaves Overwing.
   - Revise it for the client's priorities above. Keep the idea (the window).
2. Landfall (concept C in design/concepts.md, the full photo hero with the search docked across its lower edge). New, in landfall/: landfall/design/, landfall/prototype/, landfall/content/, landfall/build/, landfall/site/, landfall/references/.
   - Primary reference: Kasa (Varun's pick, https://kasa.com). It needs a full deconstruction (home and search hero, results, pinned compact search; the hotel detail is already in references/deconstruct/kasa-hotel-detail.md).
   - Donors: choose from references/shortlist.md and cards.md among sites Overwing does not use (European Sleeper, Ferryhopper, Mercury, Sidetracked). Never Hipcamp, Monocle, Faye or The Hoxton.
   - The client's three named sites (Booking.com, Expedia, Priceline) are ease-of-use benchmarks for both designs: technique only (how search, filters, price display and mobile flow work), never their look, code, text or images.
   - Type: a clean, readable family that is not Calibre and not Geist. Prefer a free licence (SIL OFL) so it can ship.
   - Palettes: Landfall's OWN navy, white and teal (different shades and roles from Overwing's), which is the default, plus Harbour gold as the alternate, re-measured on Landfall's own hero photo.

HOW TO WORK
